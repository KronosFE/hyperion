The core bet · CGYRO

Negative triangularity tames the turbulence

The heat that bleeds out of a fusion core is carried by turbulence. Flipping the plasma's triangularity negative — δ −0.30 — suppresses it. Nonlinear gyrokinetics confirm the drop: −40% at the ion scale, −80% at the electron scale.

Live · left: conventional (δ>0), turbulent, heat escaping · right: Hyperion (δ −0.30), suppressed
How the fuel is made

The breeding blanket

14 MeV neutrons stream out of the plasma, through the first wall, into a lithium blanket — where each one breeds a fresh tritium atom that returns to the fuel loop.
